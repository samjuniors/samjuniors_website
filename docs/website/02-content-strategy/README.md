## Core Content Principles

1. **Parent-Company-First**: SamJuniors is the permanent brand narrative; products are dynamic expressions.
2. **Visitor-Led Discovery**: Structure sections to answer the visitor's next logical question.
3. **Progressive Disclosure**: High-level clarity precedes deep technical specifications.
4. **Three Information Depths**: Instant, Understand, and Deep Dive tiers (non-linear access).
5. **Strategic Product Prominence**: Prominence is curated; "featured" does not simply mean "newest."
6. **Contextual Proof System**: Multi-dimensional evidence (People, Product, Builder, Evidence) embedded contextually.
7. **One Dominant Cognitive Purpose**: Each major section focuses on one primary cognitive task.
8. **Zero Fabricated Evidence**: Strict prohibition against unverified testimonials, metrics, or logos.
9. **Future Without Hype**: Clear differentiation between vision, committed roadmap, and active exploration.
10. **Scalable Multi-Product Architecture**: Content models support future products without architectural redesign.
