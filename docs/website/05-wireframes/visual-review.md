## 3. HUMAN-001 & Design Test Evaluation

1. **Distinctiveness Test**: Uses refined bespoke obsidian surfaces, architectural stone accents (`#d4a373`), and restrained ice-blue highlights (`#70b8ff`). Zero generic AI startup purple gradient blobs, zero decorative floating glowing spheres.
2. **Comprehension Test**: Clear visual hierarchy allows skimmers to understand the company in 10 seconds, while deep technical details expand on intent.
3. **Complexity Test**: All motion and interactivity (such as the Lumora Workbench switcher) directly serve comprehension and demonstrate tool performance.
4. **Multi-Product Test**: The parent brand remains permanent at the top and bottom, while Lumora occupies dedicated spotlight staging that can rotate as future platforms mature.
5. **Mobile Test**: Re-composed independently with fluid vertical rhythm and zero horizontal swipe traps.
6. **Truth Test**: Zero fabricated metrics, zero fake testimonials, zero unvetted claims.
