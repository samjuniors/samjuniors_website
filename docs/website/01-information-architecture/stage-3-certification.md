## 2. Quality Gate Verification

All 8 mandatory quality gates from the [Architecture Manifesto](file:///d:/Projects/SamjuniorsWebsite/docs/website/architecture-manifesto.md) have been evaluated:

- [x] **30-Second Rule**: Immediate comprehension of purpose and Lumora flagship.
- [x] **Evidence Before Claims**: Verifiable benchmarks and proof points required for every assertion.
- [x] **Progressive Complexity**: Multi-layered information depth available on demand.
- [x] **No Dead Ends**: 100% of pages and sections provide contextual onward pathways.
- [x] **Every Scroll Rewards**: High-density insight and engaging motion pacing throughout.
- [x] **Hero Product**: Lumora prominently established at the apex of the ecosystem.
- [x] **Signature Moment**: Two signature moments specified for the homepage; mandatory for all major pages.
- [x] **Trust First**: Credibility, transparency, and authenticity established before conversion points.
